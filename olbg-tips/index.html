How to read this site

1. Picks are the bold selection in each table. The “Pick” column is the bet; market and price sit next to it. Each sport page repeats its picks at the top, then prints the full written prediction underneath.

2. Prices in this site are ours. OLBG does not publish decimal odds for most of these markets, so we originate a price from the share of tips on each selection, shrunk towards the market's base rate. The arithmetic is on the Method page and in scripts/price_model.py.

3. Confidence is a data-quality label, not a promise. High needs at least eight tips on the market and a modelled chance of 62% or better. Single-tip “100%” selections are never high.

4. Everything unresolved is on Flagged for review with source links.

Every market row links to the OLBG page it came from. Form numbers are only stated where an official league page was read (NHL.com, NBA.com); everything else is attributed to OLBG's published tip counts. Unconfirmed fields are left blank and listed on the Flagged for review page. This board is the tip-consensus view; the primary board covers the same sports with tournament-source pricing. Our prices are analytical output, not a bookmaker offer.
