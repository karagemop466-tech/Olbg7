How to read this page

Our own fair price is shown in the data as well as the published line. Where a fair price would fall outside the band it is floored at 1.40 or capped at 101.00 and the row is flagged band-floor-applied or band-cap-applied — nothing is quietly rounded into the band. Outright markets with large fields are priced with the official field size where one was published (120 for the Mexico Open, 54 for the Simmons Bank Championship, 128 for Players Championship 31) and with a conservative 1/32 prior where it was not.
