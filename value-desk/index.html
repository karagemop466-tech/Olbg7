Executive summary — where today's confidence sits

Milwaukee's Game 4 money line is the strongest baseball play on the card: MLB's official schedule has the Brewers 3-0 up after a 10-1 win in which Robert Gasser allowed one hit over six innings, and both OLBG tips back them again. In cricket, India have won the first two T20Is by four wickets each and carry 35 of 49 tips into Wellington. Kimi Antonelli won the United States Grand Prix from pole and can wrap up the title in Mexico, while Mark Selby (4th in the official WST rankings) and SC Magdeburg (5-0 in EHF Group F) anchor the snooker and handball cards.

The value end of the card is deliberately long: Wyndham Clark is the highest-ranked player in a 120-man Mexico Open field but our line is longer than the +1200 price the PGA TOUR itself quoted; Nathan Aspinall shares the Players Championship 31 tip lead at a big price; and New Zealand are written off at 35-49 in the third T20I despite scoring 225 and 180 in the two narrow losses. Every one of those picks sits between −250 and +10000 — the band the brief asks us to focus on — rather than being a short-priced favourite dressed up as a certainty.
