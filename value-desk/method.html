How every line was made

No bookmaker price is copied, no probability is invented to fill a gap, and no external handicap is quoted. These are our own originating lines. The scripts that produce them are tools/value_build.py and tools/value_site.py; the raw inputs are data/value/capture.json and data/value/extra_markets.json.

1. Capture

OLBG publishes a public hub per sport at https://www.olbg.com/betting-tips/{Sport}/{id} and an event page at .../All_Events/{Event}/{id}?event_id={id}. The 143 hub rows for 27 October were transcribed line by line by session arena-e0f59668; this session independently re-fetched seven hubs to spot-check them and read event pages directly for golf, baseball, darts, motor racing, snooker and greyhounds. A further 50 rows came from those event pages and official competition sites. Each row keeps the exact tip split, event label and URL.

2. Originating a price

A tip split w/n is not a probability. We shrink it toward a structural prior:

p = (w + k × prior) / (n + k)
published decimal = 1 / (p × 1.05), floored at 1.40 and capped at 101.00
  • Two-way markets (money line, win match, handicap, totals, yes/no): prior 1/2, k = 6.
  • Three-way markets (handball rows where OLBG displayed 'Tie'): prior 1/3, k = 6.
  • Race winners (six-dog greyhound races): prior 1/6, adjusted to 1/4 or 1/5 where the official racecard shows vacant traps, using OLBG's published Romford trap-1 rate of 19.4%.
  • Outrights with a published field size: prior 1/N with N = 120 (Mexico Open, PGA TOUR), 54 (Simmons Bank Championship, official tournament site) or 128 (Players Championship 31, PDC), and k = clamp(ceil(N/8), 6, 12).
  • Outrights without a published field size: conservative prior 1/32, k = 6, flagged field-size-default-32. The field size is never guessed as a fact.
  • No tip split: a documented prior from official data (ranking gap, series state, group standings, trap draw) with the basis named on the row, or an equal-chance structural prior.

The 1.05 factor is our margin. Prices shorter than 1.40 (−250) are floored, prices longer than 101.00 (+10000) are capped, the unrounded fair price stays in the data and both cases are flagged. That keeps every published line inside the band the brief specifies without pretending a 1/10 shot is a value bet.

3. Confidence

Confidence measures the strength of the evidence, not the implied chance of winning — the brief explicitly asks for high-confidence picks out to +10000, not just 70%+ favourites.

  • High: price in band, fair p ≥ 0.07, at least two sourced facts and at least one from an official competition site or OLBG, with the author judging the evidence strong.
  • Medium: price in band, at least one official/OLBG source, but thinner evidence, a small tip sample or a long-dated event.
  • Low: structural-prior-only prices, longshots, or rows whose facts could not be checked against an official source.

The rule is applied in tools/value_build.py, so a label cannot be inflated by hand. 30 high / 41 medium / 14 low resulted.

4. Verification rules

  • Every table row — pick, line, source and flag — carries at least one https source URL. Open the sources page to review all 59 of them.
  • If a value cannot be confirmed from an official source it is left blank: bookmaker odds, injury/team news, exact start timezone and unverified field sizes are all blank rather than filled.
  • Secondary sources (aggregators, wiki, tipster comments) are labelled and flagged on the row. Nothing from them is presented as an official statistic.
  • Every prediction is at least 30 words (ours are 53-73), unique, names the selection and its price, and avoids the words “model” and “verified”.
  • tools/value_check.py re-checks all of the above and internal link integrity; the site is only published when it exits clean.

5. Known limits

OLBG's retrievable HTML does not include bookmaker odds, so there is no price comparison. The snooker and greyhound hubs had no tips at all on 27 October; their rows come from WST and Romford official publications and are not OLBG community picks. Tip samples as small as 1/1 are common and are flagged. Ante-post markets for 2027 are inherently uncertain. Handball hub rows that display 'Tie' as the most-tipped outcome are treated as a display artefact and priced as three-way contests. All 221 findings are on the flagged page.
