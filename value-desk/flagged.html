Prices shorter than -250 or longer than +10000 are floored or capped

The coverage brief asks for high-confidence picks between -250 and +10000. Where our fair price falls outside that range the published line is floored at 1.40 or capped at 101.00, the fair price is retained in the data, and the row is flagged band-floor-applied or band-cap-applied. Nothing is silently rounded into the band.
