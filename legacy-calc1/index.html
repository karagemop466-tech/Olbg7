How to read the picks. Confidence is computed from our fair probability, the size of the tip sample and the price band, not typed in by hand. A pick only earns high if the fair probability is above 70%, the market has at least 8 tips, and the fair decimal price sits inside the requested −250 to +10000 band (1.40 to 101.00). The band is tested on the fair price rather than the margined card, so the 5% margin cannot disqualify a qualifying pick.
