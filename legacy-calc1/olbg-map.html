How OLBG is put together

Every page we read, the URL pattern it follows, and what it actually returned on 2026-10-27. This is the map we work from when we refresh the board.

URL grammar

sport_roothttps://www.olbg.com/betting-tips/{Sport}/{sport_id}
competition_pagehttps://www.olbg.com/betting-tips/{Sport}/{Group}/{Competition}/{sport_id}

event_pagehttps://www.olbg.com/betting-tips/{Sport}/{Group}/{Competition}/{Event_Slug}/{sport_id}?event_id={id}
tipster_pagehttps://www.olbg.com/best-tipsters/{Sport}/{sport_id}
Notes from the crawl
  • The trailing number is the sport id and is identical on every page for that sport.
  • The event_id query parameter is required. Slugs alone are ambiguous and the same slug can appear under different groups.
  • Some listings use a group segment of All_{Sport} (Rugby Union, Volleyball, Handball, Darts, Motor Racing, Greyhounds, Gaelic, Snooker) and some use real groups (Basketball/Euroleague, Ice_Hockey/NHL/NHL, Baseball/MLB/MLB).
  • Guessing a wrong sport id returns a hard 404, e.g. /betting-tips/Tennis/6 - the correct id is 3.
  • Event pages expose every market with its line and per-outcome tip split; listing pages expose only the most-tipped selection.
  • Bookmaker prices are client-side rendered and were absent from every page retrieved on 2026-10-27 (flag F-001).
  • On 2026-10-27 the Snooker (id 8) and Greyhounds (id 28) roots and all-events pages returned 'No tips found'.

What the event pages give you that the listings do not

A sport listing shows one row per event: the most-tipped selection, its market, the split (e.g. 9/16) and the comment count. An event page breaks the same match into every market OLBG carries - money line, pointspread or handicap with the actual line (e.g. CLE −4.50, Under 232.50), game totals - and gives the split for each outcome, plus the tipster's written comment. That is the page to read when pricing more than the favourite. Bookmaker prices are rendered client-side and were absent from every page we retrieved, which is why this project originates its own lines instead of quoting OLBG's.
