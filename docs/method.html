Pricing method (originated lines)

  1. Fixture first: Only fixtures confirmed on an official league/tournament/federation page (or a trusted schedule reproducing it) are listed. Each row links to that page.
  2. Estimate probability: We weigh (in order) confirmed rankings/seeding, confirmed recent results in the same competition, group/league record and goal/points difference, home advantage, and — only if a cited source confirms it — team news. No other inputs.
  3. Fair price: Fair decimal odds = 1 ÷ estimated probability (e.g. 70% → 1.43, about −233 American). We round to two decimals. This is a no-margin fair line, so a bookmaker will usually offer shorter odds; if the bookmaker is longer than our fair price, that is the value signal — we do not copy bookmaker odds as our own.
  4. Markets checked per sport: Match winner, handicap/spread where a totals/handicap angle was stronger (labelled as Match Total / Top-20 / Podium / Series markets), totals (runs/goals/points), and tournament/series outrights. The market used is printed in every row.
  5. Confidence: High = estimated ≥70% (roughly −233 or shorter), Medium = 55–69%, Low = <55%. Outright winners in 32–128 player fields are Low even for favourites, which is why most High picks are match winners, top-finishes or series markets. Most offered prices fall between −250 and +10000 as requested; fair prices for chances above about 71% are mathematically shorter than −250, so the few shortest High prices (down to −909 at 90%) are shown with their exact conversion and should be treated as probability markers, not value bets.
  6. What we never do: Invent a fixture, opponent, trap draw, county champion, probable pitcher, ranking gap or injury. If it is not in the linked source, the cell stays blank and the row moves to Flagged for Review.
