Brief contradiction: >70% AND no shorter than -250-250 is a 71.4% implied probability, so a selection cannot be both 'greater than 70%' and 'no shorter than -250' outside a 70.0%-71.4% band. A second condition, >70% chance, cannot be met by any price longer than about +43.Price window -250/+10000 is applied to every pick and shown as a tag. Confidence is graded on probability separately, and Tier B reference lines are capped at Medium. Result: 3 High picks (Szeged 1.39, Veszprem 1.26, WAS Wizards 1.40); only WAS Wizards is inside the -250/+10000 window because the other two price shorter than -250. No pick is claimed to satisfy the literal 70.0-71.4% band.www.olbg.com
