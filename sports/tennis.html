Djokovic to beat Humbert in his Basel return (1.50)

medium grade B our line 1.50 / -200 implied 66.7% OLBG vote 4/5 (80%) 2-way

Novak Djokovic plays the Swiss Indoors for the first time since 2011 and opens against Ugo Humbert, a man he beat in their only previous meeting at Wimbledon 2019. He is 12-2 at this tournament, won it on his debut in 2009 and was runner-up a year later, and his season is being run around a place at the ATP Finals - the live Race to Turin has him 11th on 2,840 points with 3,340 as a theoretical ceiling. Four of five OLBG tips are on him and we price the match win at 1.50 (-200) for a centre-court start in Basel.
