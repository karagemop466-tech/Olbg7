Flags touching Handball

U-002 · Handball draw/tie clustering across 11 of 15 priced handball markets

Handball defect

The EHF Champions League markets on the OLBG card are overwhelmingly tipped on the 'Tie' outcome (PSG v Aalborg 7/9, Wisla Plock v Nantes 8/11, Magdeburg v Kriens-Luzern 9/14, Vardar v Melsungen 8/13, Celje v Zagreb 6/8, Kielce v Kolstad 7/11 and more). Handball draws are rare: the EHF's own group-phase table shows only one drawn result across the matches already played in these groups (Aalborg 33-33 PSG). A line built from those votes would imply a 40-60% draw chance, so every handball Tie selection is graded C, capped at low confidence and excluded from the high-confidence set; the team-side prices are published instead, with the clustering named on each row.

Do not stake on any Tie line on this board.
