How every number on this site is made

Two questions decide the whole board: what is the chance, and is the price worth taking. Neither is typed by hand. This page states the arithmetic so you can check it.

2. CAL-4C — the consensus price

OLBG prints, for each market, how many tips each selection received. That is a small, biased sample of opinion, so it is shrunk towards the market-structure prior before it becomes a price:

prior    = 1 / number of outcomes           (two-way 0.500, three-way 0.333, outright 0.125)
K        = 6 pseudo-tips for 8+ real tips, 12 for 4-7, 20 for 0-3
p_fair   = (tips_on_selection + K * prior) / (total_tips + K)
our price= 1 / (p_fair * 1.05)              (5% book margin)

The sample taper matters: a single tip on a three-way market prices near 0.36, not near 0.70. A two-way market is priced on both sides, so the other outcome is the complement of the printed split.

3. CAL-4R — the rating price

Used where the tip split cannot price the market we care about: handball (OLBG prints the draw as the most-tipped selection on 11 of 15 matches, so the split is not a match-winner split) and every handicap or total. Expected scoring comes from official goals/points for and against per game:

expected_home = (home_scored_per_game + away_conceded_per_game) / 2
expected_away = (away_scored_per_game + home_conceded_per_game) / 2
margin        = expected_home - expected_away,  normal with a sport-specific sigma
total line    = expected_home + expected_away
cover / over  = normal tail from the same mean and sigma

4. CAL-4P — the league-average prior

Five handball fixtures have no club-specific table line retrieved. Rather than leave them blank or invent a rating, they are priced from the group-phase average goals per side, flagged as a prior, and no pick is ever built on one. They appear on Every line marked CAL-4P.

5. Confidence

LabelRule
highFair chance above 70% and at least three published tips. This is the brief's own definition, applied without rounding up: 70% is 1.43 decimal, so every high pick is also inside the requested band.
medium55%+ fair chance with either three tips or one independent sourced fact.
lowEverything else, including any row built on the league-average prior.

Confidence is not a claim of certainty and it is not a tipster track record. It is a statement about how much evidence sits behind a price.

6. The price band

The brief asks for selections between −250 and +10000 American odds, which is 1.40 to 101.00 decimal, or a fair chance between about 1% and 71%. Every price carries an in band or outside band label. Short favourites still appear when they are the honest answer, but they are never counted among the high-confidence headline picks.

7. What is deliberately absent

  • Bookmaker prices. OLBG renders its odds client-side; no odds were present in the retrieved pages, so no comparison is claimed and every price here is ours.
  • Injuries and team news. No official injury report was retrievable for these fixtures in this pass, so no prediction makes an injury claim.
  • Timezones. OLBG prints none. Times are copied verbatim and the conflicts are listed on Flagged for review.
