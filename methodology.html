Methodology, and the rules we did not bend

1. What was captured

Twelve OLBG tip pages were fetched on 2026-10-27 between 05:30 and 05:50 UTC (Basketball 4, Ice Hockey 13, Tennis 3, Rugby Union 9, Cricket 7, Volleyball 21, Baseball 12, Handball 20, Greyhounds 28, Snooker 8, Golf 5, plus Gaelic, Motor Racing and Rugby League listings), and six OLBG event pages were then opened to read the other side of each market: MIL @ TB (160799), BOS @ CAR (200104), PSG v Aalborg (12134), Wisla Plock v Nantes (12138), South Africa v Australia (188835) and the F1 outright (912). That produced 152 unique market rows after de-duplication, of which 141 carry a tip split we can price.

OLBG prints, for each market: the selection, "X / Y Win Tips", the share, a comment count and an event link. It does not print a bookmaker price in the retrievable HTML — the odds widget is rendered client-side. So the tip split is the only numeric market signal available, and every price on this board is derived from it (flag U-001).

2. How a line is originated

p_fair = (tips_on_pick + K × prior) / (tips_total + K)     K = 6.0
decimal line = 1 / (p_fair × (1 + 0.05))     margin = 5%
American = (d−1)×100 if d≥2 else −100/(d−1)

The K pseudo-tips are the whole trick: a market with 2 tips and 2 tips on one side would otherwise read as a 100% probability, so it is pulled back to 0.625 with a 2-way prior and capped at medium confidence. Priors are 1/2 for two-way markets, 1/3 where OLBG lists a draw or tie as an outcome (all EHF handball money lines, and the cricket Test, where a "Win Match" market and a "Draw" row both appear — a limited-overs match does not get a 1/3 prior because it cannot be drawn), and 1/8 for outrights because OLBG does not print the field size (U-004).

3. Grades, confidence and the focus band

GradeMeaningEffect
A8+ tips counted on the market and no structural anomalyeligible for high confidence
B3 to 7 tipseligible for medium only
Cunder 3 tips, a degenerate 0%/100% split, or a 3-way draw cluster high confidence blocked; shown with the defect named

High confidence additionally requires a price inside the brief's −250 to +10,000 focus band (1.4–101.0 decimal) and a post-shrinkage probability of at least 60%. That is why the one-sided favourites are not the headline: a 94% vote prices at 1.16, which is outside the band by construction, and the brief asks for high-confidence work inside the band rather than a list of 1.10s.

4. Where we intervened in the data

  • Handball. Eleven of fifteen priced handball markets are voted onto the "Tie" selection (PSG v Aalborg 7/9, Plock v Nantes 8/11, Magdeburg 9/14…). The EHF's own group tables show a single drawn match in thirty played in these groups, so those selections are graded C, capped at low confidence, and the team-side prices are published instead. Flag U-002.
  • Cricket. The Test match market is priced 3-way because OLBG prints a Draw row alongside it; the Draw is quoted at 2.67 rather than being folded into a coin flip.
  • Motor racing. The Antonelli "Top 6 finish" split is carried forward from the 26 October capture and labelled as such; the OLBG comment thread on the outright quotes points totals that contradict the official Formula 1 timing sheet, and we used the official one. Flags U-006, U-007.
  • Times. OLBG's printed card times are on a US-Eastern clock and are wrong for North American fixtures in this capture (Game 4 printed 21:00 for an 8:00pm ET first pitch; NHL printed 20:07 for a 20:00 puck drop). Every time in the copy comes from the governing body instead. Flag U-010.
