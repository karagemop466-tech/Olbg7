Methodology

How to read this desk

A cautious, reproducible publishing method for a manually researched and time-limited snapshot.

What was checked

OLBG sport hubs and selected event pages were manually inspected beginning 27 Oct 2026 · 05:23 UTC. Event-specific official competition sources corroborate the schedules shown in the main event table. A source link supports a stated fact; it does not certify future lineups, withdrawals, event status or bookmaker terms.

The watchlist is deliberately separate. Its generic official-body links are research starting points, not evidence that a particular listing exists. Some retained entries are older captures from 26 October; others were reported in a concurrent 27 October capture and have not been individually rechecked here. Neither set is asserted to remain available.

Our predictions and confidence

Each note is an independent editorial direction using the cited form and schedule information. Medium means the cited case looks stronger relative to the other notes in this snapshot; low indicates a tentative preference or a wide field. These labels are subjective, are not calibrated win probabilities, and should not be read as evidence of value. No high-confidence priced picks were established.

Prices and the requested range

A current quoted American bookmaker price between −250 and +10000 is a market-price criterion, not a win-probability or confidence criterion. No current executable quote was established in this snapshot. We therefore leave both the current market price and our numeric line blank. A single 26 October FanDuel opening price cited by the PGA Tour is clearly labelled as historical, not a current offer. We make no claim of an edge.

Earlier boards converted tip shares using arbitrary priors and overrides. Tip counts are not independent outcomes or calibrated probabilities; those historic figures are not used in this desk. Read the archive warning.

Time and expiry

Official times were converted to UTC when a timezone was clear. Where a match follows earlier contests or only a date is available, the exact start is blank. A conservative earlier cutoff is used solely to stop displaying the note. The browser also hides all scheduled rows and notes at 28 Oct 2026 · 05:23 UTC. It uses the device clock; if JavaScript is disabled or the clock is earlier than the snapshot, the pages fail closed.

Updates and access

There is no automatic ingestion or content refresh. A GitHub-hosted probe checked OLBG's robots.txt with the declared CoverageDeskResearch agent and was denied for the three tested paths. It did not bypass the denial. A future maintainer needs an authorized, rate-limited source and must record retrieval times and failures before replacing this snapshot.

Rebuild and checks

The canonical research is data/current.json. Run python3 tools/build_current.py to render and python3 tools/check_current.py plus node --test tests/freshness.test.js to test. The checks prove structure, expiry behavior, internal links and source presence, not that external facts are automatically true or that quotes remain available.
