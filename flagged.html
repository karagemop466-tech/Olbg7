Flagged for review

20 flags raised on the 2026-10-27 cycle, plus every field we deliberately left blank. Nothing on this page is a hidden problem — each item says what we found, what we did about it and what the reader should not rely on.

Structural defects and conflicts

U-001 · No bookmaker prices exist in retrievable OLBG HTML

All structural

Every decimal and American price on this site is originated by us from OLBG's printed tip split (X of Y Win Tips) using the CAL-1R method: a per-market prior, K=6 pseudo-tips of shrinkage, and a 5% margin. No sportsbook quote is reproduced anywhere on the board, and none should be assumed. OLBG renders its odds widgets client-side, so they are not in the HTML that can be read.

Read prices as our line, never as the market's.

OLBG tip pages

U-002 · Handball draw/tie clustering across 11 of 15 priced handball markets

Handball defect

The EHF Champions League markets on the OLBG card are overwhelmingly tipped on the 'Tie' outcome (PSG v Aalborg 7/9, Wisla Plock v Nantes 8/11, Magdeburg v Kriens-Luzern 9/14, Vardar v Melsungen 8/13, Celje v Zagreb 6/8, Kielce v Kolstad 7/11 and more). Handball draws are rare: the EHF's own group-phase table shows only one drawn result across the matches already played in these groups (Aalborg 33-33 PSG). A line built from those votes would imply a 40-60% draw chance, so every handball Tie selection is graded C, capped at low confidence and excluded from the high-confidence set; the team-side prices are published instead, with the clustering named on each row.

Do not stake on any Tie line on this board.

U-003 · Reliability grades applied to every priced line

All method

Grade A: 8 or more tips counted on the market and no structural anomaly. Grade B: 3 to 7 tips. Grade C: fewer than 3 tips, a degenerate 0%/100% split, or a 3-way draw-cluster artefact. High confidence is blocked on any grade C line and requires at least 8 tips, a price inside the -250 to +10000 focus band and a consensus probability of at least 60% after shrinkage.

Grade is printed next to every line on the sport pages.

U-004 · Outright field sizes are assumed, not printed

All outrights method

OLBG's outright markets (F1 title-without-Antonelli, PDC World Championship, Six Nations 2027, State of Origin 2027, Super League and NRL grand finals, GAA All-Ireland championships, golf winners, man-of-the-match markets) do not print the number of entries in the field. The pricing assumes an eight-way field for a uniform prior, which moves the price of a lightly-tipped runner by more than any other single assumption in the method.

Outright prices are directional only.

U-010 · OLBG's printed start times are unreliable for North American fixtures

Ice Hockey / Baseball defect

OLBG prints its card times on a US-Eastern clock, and the pattern was confirmed against four non-US fixtures (a URC kick-off printed 15:45 for 19:45 UK, the Afghanistan v Zimbabwe T20I printed 10:30 for 14:30 GMT, Pakistan v Sri Lanka printed 04:00 for 08:00 GMT, Aalborg v Wisla Plock printed 13:45 for 18:45 local). Against official listings the North American games then come out wrong: World Series Game 4 is printed 21:00 for an 8:00pm ET first pitch, and the NHL games print 20:07 / 19:07 for 8:00pm and 7:00pm ET puck drops. The printed minutes are not used anywhere in the copy; official times are quoted instead.

Times on every row come from the governing body, never from the OLBG card.
