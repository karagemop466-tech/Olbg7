What is deliberately left blank

  • No bookmaker prices are quoted anywhere on this site, because none could be read from OLBG. Every price shown is ours.
  • Handball: no 'Tie' selections are published and no handball split is used, because the printed splits look like a display defect.
  • Handball: Kriens Luzern's and Kolstad's group records are left blank rather than estimated.
  • Snooker: no events, no lines, no picks - OLBG returned no tips.
  • Gaelic club matches: no team news or form data was available from an official source, so those two picks make no claim about players, injuries or recent results.
  • Volleyball, Handball, Greyhounds and Darts picks make no injury claims; no official team-news source exists for those fixtures at this level.
  • No weather, referee, surface-speed or travel-distance numbers are stated unless a source link on the same card supports them.
  • Rugby League World Cup fixtures (England v PNG 30 Oct, Lebanon v France 1 Nov, Tonga v Samoa 1 Nov) are listed as confirmed events but are not priced, because OLBG carries no market for them.

Method. Lines are originated by this desk from OLBG's published tip splits using the CAL-1 engine described on the pricing method page; they are not bookmaker prices. Predictions are editorial. Every factual claim carries a source link on the row or card it appears in.
