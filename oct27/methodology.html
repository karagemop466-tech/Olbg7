How We Originate Betting Lines Across All 15 Sports

OLBG’s server-rendered HTML exposes event IDs, market types, handicap/total lines and community tip counts, but omits bookmaker odds. Every price on this site is computed by our own four-family mathematical origination suite.

2. Origination Margin & Objective Confidence Rules (−250 to +10000)

Why heavy >71% chalk is never labelled High Confidence here: A raw win chance above 71.4% converts to fair odds shorter than −250 (1.40 decimal). Rather than listing −500 to −2000 money-line favourites as High Confidence, our engine caps out-of-band chalk and routes conviction plays through pointspreads, handicaps, set betting, or tournament markets priced strictly inside −250 to +10000 (1.40 ≤ price_dec ≤ 101.00).
  • Origination Overround: 4.5% on 2-way match/spread/total lines, 5.5% on 3-way 1X2 lines, and 8.0% on multi-runner outrights: price_dec = round(1 / (p_fair × overround), 2).
  • High Confidence: Requires price strictly inside [1.40, 101.00] (−250..+10000), official governing-body form/table/entry support, and either p_fair ≥ 61.5% (≥59.5% with confirmed injury/standings edge) on match/spread/total markets or ≥3.5× uniform field share with p_fair ≥ 14.5% on outrights/podiums.
  • Medium Confidence: p_fair ≥ 53.5% on head-to-head/spread/total markets or ≥1.7× uniform field share on outrights (plus heavy chalk shorter than −250).
  • Low Confidence: Marginal edges (p_fair < 53.5%), tip-shrinkage-only domestic lines without official league differentials, or earlier-morning in-play exclusions.
