F-19All OLBG prints start times in US Eastern, not local time
The printed start time is not the local kick-off. Four fixtures with a confirmed official start line up exactly when four hours are subtracted in October (US Eastern Daylight Time): Afghanistan v Zimbabwe is 18:30 Gulf time, printed 10:30; Namibia v Ireland is 09:30 in Windhoek, printed 03:30; Pakistan v Sri Lanka is 13:00 in Lahore, printed 04:00; Osaka v Paolini is 17:00 in Tokyo, printed 04:00. The Cork Senior A final on 1 November is 14:00 local and printed 09:00, five hours earlier, which matches the switch to Eastern Standard Time that day. US fixtures are close to Eastern but not always exact (Brewers at Rays is 20:00 Eastern and printed 21:00). Every time on this site is therefore copied exactly as OLBG prints it and labelled as such; no conversion is applied anywhere.
