How to read a pick

1. The selection comes first

Every prediction opens with the bet in plain words: the team or player, the market, and our price. You should never have to hunt for what is being backed.

2. The evidence is attached

Each pick lists the facts behind it with a link to the source: the official league table, the governing body, or OLBG's own listing.

3. Confidence is computed

High, medium and low are set by our fair chance, the size of the tip sample and whether an independent source stands behind the fixture — never by how the paragraph reads. The rule is on the method page.
