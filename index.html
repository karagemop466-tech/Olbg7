The 70%+ club — every line at a 70 per cent chance or better

The brief asks for high-confidence picks at a 70 per cent chance or better and for picks inside the -250 to +10000 band. Those two asks pull against each other: a 70 per cent chance is -233 on fair odds and roughly -250 once any margin is applied, so almost everything the tables make a 70 per cent favourite prices shorter than the floor. Both lists are published rather than one being quietly dropped: the pick table above uses a 64 per cent threshold so that it can stay inside the band, and this table shows every line that clears 70 per cent.
