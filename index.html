How the numbers were made

One formula, applied to every market

p_fair = (tips_on_pick + K × prior) / (tips_total + K), K = 6
our line = 1 / (p_fair × 1.05)

prior is 1/2 for two-way markets, 1/3 where a draw is a listed outcome, 1/8 for outrights (field size is not printed — flagged U-004). A 0%/100% split can never earn high confidence, and grade C lines are blocked from it.
