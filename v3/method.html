Two tracks, applied line by line. Nothing on this board is a bookmaker price, because OLBG renders those client-side and they are absent from the HTML we can retrieve. Every number below is originated by us, and every one of them can be reproduced from scripts/v3_pricing.py.

Track T — the tip share OLBG prints

Where OLBG publishes a tip count for a selection, the implied chance is that count shrunk towards a uniform prior worth six tips:

p = (k + A/m) / (n + A)
k tips on this selection · n tips on the market · m outcomes OLBG lists · A = 6.0 prior strength.

Fair American price: if p ≥ 0.5 then -round(100p/(1-p)), otherwise +round(100(1-p)/p). The shrink stops a single tip on a two-horse market reading as a near-certainty, and it keeps a 94% tip share on a sixteen-tip market from printing as an unbackable -1500.

Tiering by sample size — A n≥20 (4 markets) · B 8≤n<20 (12) · C 4≤n<8 (29) · D n<4 (56) · R our own origination (14).

Track R — our own origination

Where an official competition table, ranking or points record exists, we build the price from that instead. For a two-sided match:

d = margin_home - margin_away + HFA
p_home = 1 / (1 + 10^(-d/s))
margin is the official points (or goals, or runs) difference per game · HFA is the home-field allowance · s is the sport's spread, chosen so that one game's worth of scoring margin maps to a realistic change of chance.

Handball adds an explicit draw share of 0.0667, because the official EHF group tables record two draws across the thirty group-phase games played in groups A, C and F. Tennis is priced straight off the official ATP points ratio, p = r/(1+r). F1 podium finishes are the official season podium count divided by the 18 rounds completed — the podium counts across the top eight drivers sum to 54, which is exactly 3 × 18, so that denominator is not guessed. Golf outrights weight each entrant by one over their official world ranking, normalised across the confirmed field, with the 99 unranked entrants carrying a stated notional ranking of 150.

Where OLBG tips exist we keep the Track T price as the headline and attach the Track R line alongside it in the lines column of every row. Where a row is Track R primary — the Mexico Open outright — the tip-derived number is superseded and the row says so.

The confidence gates

HIGH requires all five:

  1. our fair price sits between fair price between -250 and +10000 inclusive inclusive;
  2. at least one official source corroborates the direction of the pick;
  3. the row is not hit by a known OLBG display defect;
  4. sample — match markets need eight or more tips, or a Track R line built from an official competition table; outrights need (joint-)most-tipped status and to be an officially leading contender;
  5. probability — match markets p ≥ 0.55; outrights p ≥ min(0.40, 2.5/m), or, where five or fewer live contenders are priced from an official points table, the leader of that table.

MEDIUM is the same direction with a price outside the band or a sample below the threshold. LOW is a pick whose argument is sound but where a value it depends on could not be confirmed from an official source, so that value is left blank. Result across the slate: 19 high, 36 medium, 10 low.

What we refuse to do

  • No inference. If a venue, a competition, a field size or an injury cannot be sourced, the cell is blank and the gap appears on Flagged for Review. Five picks on this board carry no price at all for exactly that reason.
  • No padding. Snooker and Greyhounds carry zero picks because OLBG carries zero events for either on this date. We do not invent fixtures to hit a target count.
  • No borrowed language. The words “model” and “verified” do not appear anywhere in prediction prose; the machinery is internal and is described on this page instead. scripts/v3_picks.py rejects any pick that uses them.
  • No over-fitting. The baseball money line is deliberately not originated from the three-game World Series sample: a logistic fit to those three results returned 0.982, or -4200, which describes the past rather than the next game. We publish OLBG's tip-derived line and flag it. The three-game totals are still usable for the run total, because a mean is a mean.
  • No nonsense prices. An earlier attempt to price every F1 title contender on the gap to the leader emitted +2479913 for Oscar Piastri. Contenders more than two race wins — 50 points — behind the best-placed eligible rival are excluded, and the exclusion is stated on the page.
  • No borrowed labels. Where OLBG misfiles a competition — the Maybank Championship under the DP World Tour although it is an LPGA event, or Fuchse Berlin v RK Partizan under the EHF European League although both clubs sit in the official Champions League group A table — we record the correct competition and flag the difference rather than repeat it.

The OLBG clock

OLBG prints times in US Eastern. Four independent fixtures confirm it on the same day: Auckland v Canterbury prints 17:30 against an official 10:30am NZT start on 28 October, which is 5:30pm EDT on the 27th; Fr Caseys v Kildimo-Pallaskenry prints 10:30 against an official 15:30 Irish start; the URC round-6 fixtures align on the same shift; and all 115 swept rows shift by the same offset from their official local times. Three rows still conflict after the offset is applied — Brewers at Rays, Afghanistan v Zimbabwe and South Africa v Australia — and those stay on the flagged page.
