How to interpret the existing picks

Use the dashboard and the linked sport pages only as archived research from the date above. The shown originating prices are derived estimates from historical OLBG tip splits, not live bookmaker odds. The predictions are not updated for results, withdrawals, injuries, line-ups, weather or market movement. The original gaps and conflicting data are listed on Flagged for review.

To publish a genuinely current card, a fresh OLBG capture and re-check of each official/statistical source is required. Until that is done, this site intentionally makes no claim to provide today's actionable picks.
